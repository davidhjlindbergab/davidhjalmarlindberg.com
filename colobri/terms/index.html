Trying Colobri

Colobri is free to try for 7 days from the first time it opens, in each browser and in each editor. During the trial every feature works. After it, Colobri still opens frames and composes and shows looks, but exporting LUTs, stills and clips, and applying looks in an editor, need a licence. The licence check

When you add a key, Colobri checks it with Lemon Squeezy’s licence service, and checks it again about once a week when you are online. In between, Colobri works offline. A key that has been refunded, or that is used on more installs than its limit, stops unlocking Colobri. To move to another computer, remove the key in Colobri on the old one first: open the licence and choose Remove from this computer.
