Terms for Nyans

The short version: try Nyans free for 7 days, then a licence is €59 once. It is yours to use on up to two computers, for every Nyans 1.x version, and you can have your money back within 14 days.

Who we are

Nyans is made by David HJ Lindberg AB, a company in Sweden (“we”). You can reach us at david@nakaya.world.

Trying Nyans

Nyans is free to try for 7 days from the first time it opens, in each browser and in each editor. During the trial every feature works. After it, Nyans still opens frames and composes and shows looks, but exporting LUTs, stills and clips, and applying looks in an editor, need a licence. Looks you applied during the trial stay on your clips.

Buying a licence

A licence costs €59 once, VAT included. There is no subscription. Orders are handled by Lemon Squeezy, our reseller and merchant of record: Lemon Squeezy takes the payment, charges the right VAT for your country, sends your receipt, and its buyer terms apply to the payment. Your licence key is in the receipt e-mail.

What the licence lets you do

The licence is personal: each person who uses Nyans needs their own. Please don’t share, publish or resell your key. You may not copy, sell or share Nyans itself, or decompile or modify it, except where the law allows you to.

The licence check

When you add a key, Nyans checks it with Lemon Squeezy’s licence service, and checks it again about once a week when you are online. In between, Nyans works offline. A key that has been refunded, or that is used on more installs than its limit, stops unlocking Nyans. To move to another computer, remove the key in Nyans on the old one first: open the licence and choose Remove from this computer.
