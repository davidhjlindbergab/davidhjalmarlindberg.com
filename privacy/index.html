Statistics

Visits are counted without cookies.

The site counts its own page views, downloads and clicks on links. Nothing is stored on your device and nothing identifies you: no cookie, no id. For each page it notes the page, the site or link that sent you, the kind of device, your browser and system, your browser’s language and time zone (which tells the country), how long the page was in view, and a click on a download, on Buy or on a link to another site. In Colobri in your browser it also counts when a frame or a clip is opened, when looks are generated and when a look is exported, and of which kind, never anything about the images themselves. The counts are added up per day as they arrive, and only those daily totals are kept, in a spreadsheet in our Google account. After four months the days are added up per month.

The counts go to a Google Apps Script in our Google Workspace, so Google receives your IP address and browser details with each count, as with any request, and processes them for us. The count is sent without cookies, so Google can’t tie it to a Google account. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in knowing how the site is used.

To stop the counting in your browser, open davidhjalmarlindberg.com/?stats=off. That keeps a note in your browser that you asked, and ?stats=on removes it.

Cookies and the Meta pixel

On the Andrum and Colobri pages, we ask first.

Only if you accept, those pages load Meta’s pixel. It tells Meta, which runs Facebook and Instagram, that you visited, opened Colobri in your browser, downloaded a trial, asked to hear when Andrum goes on sale or started a checkout, together with your IP address, browser details and the cookies Meta sets (_fbp and _fbc). We see the results as counts and use them to run our ads. Meta may link the data to your Facebook or Instagram account and uses it under its own privacy policy. For collecting and sending these data, we and Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd are joint controllers.

The legal basis is your consent. Your answer is kept in your browser so you are not asked again, and you can change it at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of the Andrum and Colobri pages.
