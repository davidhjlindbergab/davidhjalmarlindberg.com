Privacy.

What happens to information about you when you visit this page, try Andrum or buy it. In short: Andrum itself collects nothing, this page has no analytics, and your order is handled by Lemon Squeezy.

Updated 30 October 2026

Who is responsible

David HJ Lindberg AB (company number 559291-3353), Sweden, is responsible for the personal data described here. Write to me@davidhjalmarlindberg.com with any question about it.

Andrum, the plug-in

Andrum runs on your computer and collects nothing about you, your computer or your work. It goes online once: when you paste your licence key, it reads where the licence service is (a small file on this site) and sends the key to that service, which sends back your licence. The licence holds your name, e-mail address, order number and the date of the order, and is kept on your computer. Nothing else leaves your computer: no usage statistics, no crash reports, no identifiers.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub records the IP address of visitors to keep the service secure and running, as its privacy statement describes; we don't receive those records. The site sets no cookies of its own and uses no analytics. The checkout loads from Lemon Squeezy only when you click Buy.

Buying

Lemon Squeezy (Lemon Squeezy LLC), our reseller and merchant of record, collects your name, e-mail address, billing country and payment details to take the payment, charge the right tax and send your receipt with the licence key. It processes them under its own privacy policy.

We receive the order: your name, e-mail address, country, order number and date, and the campaign name of the ad you came from, if the link you followed carried one. We use it to make your licence, to answer you, to handle refunds and for our bookkeeping, for as long as the law requires.

The licence service

When you paste your licence key into Andrum or onto the licence page, the key goes to our licence service, which runs on Cloudflare. It asks Lemon Squeezy whether the key belongs to an Andrum order and, if it does, signs your licence with the name and e-mail address of that order. The service keeps no record of the keys, licences or people it handles. Cloudflare processes the connection, including your IP address, to deliver it.

Measuring ads

We may advertise Andrum on Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google. When we do, the product page asks you once whether we may measure which ads bring visitors. Only if you allow it does it load Meta's pixel or Google's tag, which set cookies and tell Meta or Google when someone who saw an ad downloads Andrum or buys it; we see that as counts in their reports. Meta and Google process this under their own policies. If you don't allow it, nothing is loaded. You can change your choice at any time with Cookie settings at the foot of the product page.

E-mail

If you write to us, we keep the conversation for as long as we need it to help you, and for up to two years after that unless something, such as a refund, needs it longer. Our e-mail is provided by Google.

Why we may use it

  • Your order, licence and refunds: to fulfil the purchase you made.
  • Bookkeeping: because the law requires it.
  • Answering your e-mail: our legitimate interest in helping you, or the purchase.
  • Measuring ads: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
