Add your licence.

Your licence key is in the receipt Lemon Squeezy e-mailed you when you bought Andrum. Adding it takes a few seconds.

  1. Copy the licence key from your receipt.
  2. Open Andrum in your music app or video editor, and click Trial at the bottom of its window.
  3. Click Paste license. Andrum fetches your licence once and works offline from then on.

Andrum on a second computer? Paste the same key there too: one licence covers one person on up to two computers.

On a computer without internet

Get your licence here, on any phone or computer that's online, and take it to the other computer.


        

          
          
        

        
On the other computer, paste it into Andrum with Paste license, or click Open license file… and choose the file.
