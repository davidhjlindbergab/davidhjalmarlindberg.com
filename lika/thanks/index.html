Your licence key

Your licence key is on its way. It is also in the e-mail from Polar: click Access purchase there.

  1. Download Lika, if you haven't

    One zip for a Mac with Apple silicon, on macOS 13 or later, signed and notarized by Apple. Unzip it and move Lika to your Applications folder.

  2. Add the key

    In Lika, click Trial in the bar at the top (or choose Lika › Licence… in the menu), paste the key and click Add licence. Lika checks the key with Polar once, then works offline.

Download Lika for Mac

The key is also in Polar's customer portal: the receipt e-mail links to it (Access purchase). No e-mail after a few minutes? Look in your spam folder.

Moving to another Mac

On the old Mac, open Lika, choose Lika › Licence… and Remove from this computer, or free that Mac in Polar's customer portal. Then add the key on the new one.
