Your licence key is in the receipt Lemon Squeezy just e-mailed you. One key unlocks Lika on up to two Macs.

  1. Find your key

    Open the receipt for Lika. The key is one line of letters and numbers in five groups, like XXXXXXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXXXXXXXXXX.

  2. Download Lika, if you haven't

    One zip for a Mac with Apple silicon, on macOS 13 or later, signed and notarized by Apple. Unzip it and move Lika to your Applications folder.

  3. Add the key

    In Lika, click Trial in the bar at the top (or choose Lika › Licence… in the menu), paste the key and click Add licence. Lika checks the key with Lemon Squeezy once, then works offline.
