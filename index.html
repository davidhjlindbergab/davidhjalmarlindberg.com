A little about me

I’m David, a creative director,
filmmaker and artist based in Stockholm.

I work in light, sound and code: films, music, software and places you can walk into. Some of it I make with artists and organisations, and some of it is my own, like the world of Nakãya and the tools Andrum, Lika and Colobri. Either way, I’m drawn to work that makes you feel something.
