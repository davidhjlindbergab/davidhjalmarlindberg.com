About

Stockholm

For more than twelve years, David has created visuals for some of the world’s biggest artists and brands. Now he is building a world of his own.

David Hjalmar Lindberg is a Swedish creative director, filmmaker and AI artist. In 2022 he started Nakãya, an AI-born universe of creatures, landscapes and myth that won the Swedish Design Award in 2023. He speaks internationally on creativity, spirituality and the future of art in the age of AI.
