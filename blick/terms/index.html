Last updated 20 November 2026

The short version: try Blick free for 7 days. After that a subscription, by the month or by the year, keeps it working on two computers, with every update. Cancel whenever you like. What you make with it is yours.

Blick is made by David HJ Lindberg AB, a company in Sweden ("we"). You can reach us at me@davidhjalmarlindberg.com. Polar (Polar Software, Inc.) sells Blick for us as the merchant of record: it takes the payments, charges the VAT or sales tax where you live, sends the receipts and gives you your licence key.

The trial

Blick works in full, exports included, for 7 days from the first time you open it on a computer. It asks for no card and no account. After that you can still add, match, grade and look at your clips; rendering files, saving LUTs and stills, and copying CDLs need a licence.

The subscription

A subscription is paid in advance, 49 euros for a month or 495 euros for a year, tax included. It renews by itself at the end of each period until you cancel.

You can cancel at any time in Polar's customer portal, which your receipt links to. Blick then keeps working until the end of the period you have paid for, and you are not charged again.

If the price changes, we tell you by e-mail before your next period begins, so that you can cancel first.

What a subscription gives you

One subscription is for one person. You may use Blick on up to two computers of your own at a time, for your own work and for work you do for others, commercial work included. For as long as you subscribe you get every update.

A company takes one subscription for each person who uses Blick.

Your licence key

You add the key once on each computer. Blick checks it with Polar over the internet then, and about once a week afterwards. In between it works offline, for up to 30 days at a time; after that it asks for the internet once before it exports again.

To move Blick to another computer, remove the key on the old one (Blick, Licence…, Remove from this computer), or free that computer in Polar's customer portal, and add the key on the new one.

When a subscription ends, is refunded or is misused, its key stops working. Blick still opens your projects and shows your clips, and everything you have made stays on your computer; exporting needs a licence again.
