Generate analyzes the whole clip before it touches a pixel. Then it creates a grade for that picture and no other, checks it against clipping and skin, and hands you every control.

Generate Blick 3.7

One press.
Four things happen.

  1. It analyzes the clip

    The frame under the playhead and six more across the clip: where black and white sit, how much color there is and in which hues, how the light falls, whether there is skin.

  2. It knows the perfect grade

    Built into the engine is what more than 3,300 finished cinematic grades have in common: their tones, the color of their shadows and lights, their richness, their skin. Nothing is looked up. Only your clip is read.

  3. It solves the grade on your clip

    Black and white go where the picture begins to clip. Then light, contrast, color, shadows and skin are fitted, on your clip and not on a chart, and again on every other clip of the cut.

  4. It checks the result

    A grade that would burn a highlight, crush a black or turn the skin is eased until it does not. Then it is yours.

Again

Press again.
Get a new idea.

The first press creates the perfect grade for the clip. Every press after it creates a look that stands as far as it can from the ones already in the row, so you are never shown the same idea twice. The setting called Experimental decides how far a look may go.

Film Blick 3.8

Film is a tab of its own.
And a lab of its own.

46 presets in eight families, each shown as a small picture of the frame you are on. Choose one and the look keeps its grade and changes its film. Behind every preset is the same film engine, open to the last slider: a negative, a print, the lab's processes, the light round a highlight, the gate and the grain.

Negative
Daylight 50, Daylight 250, Tungsten 200, Tungsten 500, Soft 160, Soft 400, Vivid 100, Street 400, Night 800, Pastel 400
Print
Print Neutral, Print Warm, Print Cool, Soft Print, Scan
Slide
Slide Vivid 50, Slide Natural 100, Slide Warm 64, Slide Soft 200, Cross Slide
16 mm
16 Daylight, 16 Tungsten, 16 Reversal, 16 Newsreel
8 mm
Super 8 Daylight, Super 8 Home, 8 Faded
Mono
Mono Fine 100, Mono Classic 400, Mono Red, Mono Soft, Mono Sepia, Mono Cold, Mono Push 1600
Vintage
Two-Color, Three-Strip, Faded Print, Seventies, Eighties
Lab
Bleach Bypass, Half Bypass, Push 2, Pull 1, Cross Process, Flashed, Day for Night
Negative
The stock as three dye layers on an instrument you can drag, with Purity, Crosstalk and Hue shift. Exposure in stops: more light gives finer grain and richer, deeper color. Develop from Pull 1 to Push 2, the Format from 8 mm to 65 mm, Acutance, and Richness and Depth for the color itself.
Print
Density, the print's curve and its Roll-off, Printer lights, Fade, the Base and its warmth. Toning for shadows, midtones and highlights on their own, and a Split tone with its amount, hue and balance.
Process
What a lab does to a film: Silver for a bleach bypass, Flare, Cross process, Dye transfer, Two-color, Age and Night. Black and white by any amount, through no filter or a yellow, orange, red, green or blue one.
Halation
The red glow where a bright light meets the film's base, with its own color. Glow and Mist beside it, and one switch for the window.
Gate
What a projector adds: Weave, Breath, Dust and Scratches, moving with the film. One switch takes all of it away.
Grain
Amount, size, roughness, softness and color; new on every frame, slower, or still; its own weight in shadows, midtones and highlights. A loupe shows a piece of the picture at 100, 200 or 400 percent, where grain can be judged.
One switch, one Reset
A switch at the head takes all film off the look. Reset gives back exactly the film the look came with, however far you have gone.
Your own stocks
Save keeps the film you have built as a preset of your own. It joins the others as its own family, in every project.
In a LUT, as far as a LUT goes
The stock and its print travel in a .cube. Halation, the gate, grain and acutance need the picture itself, so they are in renders and stills.

Looks Blick 3.7

A library of 3,773 looks,
one click from your clip.

Looks is the third tab, beside Adjust and Film. Every look is a finished frame, shown as a picture. Press one and the engine grades the clip to it, solved on your picture. Press two times and you see the looks close to it.

Smart sorting
Relevant puts the looks that fit the frame in front of you first. Latest and Most used are the other two orders.
Six kinds
All, Dark, Bright, Warm, Cool, and Saved: the grades you have kept yourself, in the same place.
As large as you like
A Size slider goes from nine looks in a row to one. The panel widens while Looks is open, and its edge can be dragged.
Pictures or gradients
See each look as its frame, or as the two colors of its shadows and its lights.

One clip, many looks

Choose a picture.
The engine grades to it.

Each small picture is a look from the library. The large one is the same clip, graded to it.

Saved looks
Save keeps the whole grade: the look, its film, every slider, switch and curve, with a small picture. Saved looks belong to the app, so they are there in every project.
Fitted, not pasted
A saved look or a look from the library is fitted to the clip the playhead is in. The same look reads right on a night street and on a snowfield.
Out of the row again
Every look in the row has a cross. Undo puts it back.

Applies to

The whole cut, one scene,
or just this clip.

One line under the look's name says what it covers. A look for all clips is still solved on each of them. A scene can wear its own. So can a single clip, with its own sliders, and it can go back to the cut's look without losing what it had.

Scenes Blick 3.6
Select the clips that belong together and make them a scene. A scene has its own look here and its own match in Prepare, and pressing it shows its clips alone.
Skin, under every look Blick 3.5
Every look is read as it is shown, on every clip. Where skin would carry clearly more color than it should, the look gives way on the warm hues, for that clip alone.
The two ends Blick 3.5
On every clip the engine counts what stands at full black and at full white. Where a look would send clearly more there than arrived, that end is taken back, unseen on the sliders.

Adjust

Every number is yours.

A generated look is not a black box. It is a set of sliders and curves, and you can move any of them. Adjust holds five windows. Each can be enlarged, and each can be switched off to see what it does.

Tone
Exposure, Contrast, Shadows, Highlights, Blacks and Whites, with its curves. A look alone never clips what did not clip in the frame as it arrived; your hand may.
Color
Color, Warmth and Tint, Relight, Palette, Vibrance and Crossover, and the color of the shade and of the light on their own. Skin hold says how much of their own color skin tones keep under a look.
Hues
Skies, Foliage and Reds: each turned, and given more or less color, on its own. Most skin is left where it is.
Finish
Separation, Detail and Soften: what is done to the picture once the color is right.
Vignette
Amount both ways, reach, feather, falloff, roundness, stretch and angle, its weight in highlights and shadows, color, tint and hue. Its middle is placed on a small picture of the frame.
All the way Blick 3.6
The sliders go to extremes: nearly white, nearly black, one grey, four times the color. What Generate sets stays well inside; the far ends are for you.

Scopes

See what the grade did.

Seven scopes at the foot of the window, as many at once as you like, in Grade and in Prepare. Blick Scope is the app's own: color and light in one drawing, in the picture's own colors.

Blick Scope
The crown says which colors and how strong. The plume says where they sit, dark at the foot and light at the top. The spine is the color of the greys: straight up is neutral. Beside them, the tones across the frame, or through time as the clip plays.
The classics
Waveform, Parade, Vector, Histogram and CIE 1931. And Numbers: what Blick Scope measures as a table, with what is clipped and what is crushed.
Look closer, and push
Pinch, or hold Cmd and scroll, to zoom into any scope where the pointer is. In Blick Scope you can take hold of the color itself: drag the crown for Warmth and Tint, the plume for Warmth and Exposure.

No waiting on a slider Blick 3.6

All looks
One press shows every look in the row at once, on the frame you are on. Prepared, Split and Graded stand beside it, and the set plays through without a pause between clips.
