Tech specs

Everything, listed.

What Blick runs on, what it opens and what it writes, what is in each of its three parts, and what each version brought.

Requirements

Mac
A Mac with Apple silicon, on macOS 13 or later. Signed and notarized.
Windows
Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, on Intel or AMD. Not yet signed for Windows, so Windows asks before it opens Blick the first time.
Connection
None for the work: clips are read, graded and rendered on the computer. A subscription's key is checked with the store once in 30 days.

Versions

The download
Blick 3.4.2 for the Mac, Blick 3.4.2 for Windows.
3.7 Blick 3.7
The library holds 3,773 looks, and the engine learned its tables again from them. Looks is a tab of its own, with kinds, three orders and a Size slider. A clip that already reads like a finished picture keeps a share of what it has. Menus work in one motion: press, hold, let go. A project opens as it was closed, and no file is read twice.
3.6 Blick 3.6
Scenes, each with its own match and its own look. A right-click menu for the whole window. Sliders that go all the way. Names with three numbers, and a name can be changed. A slider for the size of the clips. Quicker throughout: frames are kept, a clip you have been to comes at once.
3.5 Blick 3.5
A match that holds scenes together and never crushes. Care for skin under every look, on every clip. A look on another clip never clips or crushes more than on its own. The library as pictures, with Saved as one of its kinds.
3.4
Blick for Windows. A way in and back for DaVinci Resolve, Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro and CapCut. The subscription, with seven days free.

What it opens

Files
MP4 and MOV
Codecs
H.264, HEVC, ProRes and DNxHR, at 8, 10 or 12 bits
Color
Rec. 709
Sets
Clips, a folder with every clip in it, or one file with a whole film in it, which Find cuts splits into its shots
Timelines
From DaVinci Resolve and Premiere Pro through Blick's scripts and panel, from Final Cut Pro as XML, from CapCut as the project's folder

ProRes
422 Proxy, LT, 422 and HQ, 4444 and 4444 XQ
DNxHR
SQ, HQ, HQX and 444
HEVC
10-bit, 4:2:0 or 4:2:2, in software, or on the Mac's own chip
H.264
8-bit
Lossless
FFV1 at 10 or 16 bits, and uncompressed 10-bit
CineForm
10-bit 4:2:2
Image sequences
DPX at 10 bits, TIFF and PNG at 16
Stills
PNG or JPEG, at full size
The look
A .cube LUT: the look of all clips without finish, grain and vignette
The match
A .cube and a .cdl for each clip: the match alone
Sound
Carried over from each clip into its render
Back to the editor
A sequence for DaVinci Resolve or Premiere Pro, a project as FCPXML for Final Cut Pro, a copy of the project for CapCut

Prepare

Match to
Group, Hero or Neutral, with Auto match. Clips that look alike are held together as one scene Blick 3.5
Strengths
Blacks, Whites, Shoulder, Mids, Balance, Warmth, Saturation, Black point, and the spread across the clips
Scenes
A scene you make has its own match here and its own look in Grade Blick 3.6
Finish
Hold, Clean-up, Deband, Sharpen; Upscale 1.5 times, 2 times or to UHD; for the cut or for one clip
A clip's own
Trims for one clip, on top of the match
Cuts
Find cuts, Cut here, Join
Check
Pulsing level, noise, banding skies, clipped tones, repeated frames, a cut inside a clip, a hand that changes shape, clips off the set's frame rate or size
Views
Clips or Viewer; Original, Split, Prepared; zoom to the pixel; full screen

Grade

Generate
Reads seven frames across the clip. The first look is the grade that suits the clip best; each further look stands apart from the ones before it. One setting, Experimental, for how far a look may go
Library
3,773 looks as pictures or gradients; All, Dark, Bright, Warm, Cool and Saved; Relevant, Latest or Most used; a Size slider Blick 3.7
Applies to
All clips, one scene, or only this clip
Tone
Amount, Exposure, Contrast, Shadows, Highlights, Blacks, Whites
Color
Color, Warmth, Tint, Relight, Palette, Skin hold, Vibrance, Crossover, Shade color, Light color
Hues
Skies, Foliage and Reds, each with its turn and its color
Film
Three dyes with movable peaks, Density, Print curve, Develop from Pull 1 to Push 2, Silver, Flare, Cross process, Dye transfer, Two-color, Age, Night
Finish
Glow, Separation, Detail, Halation, Mist, Soften
Grain
Amount, size, roughness, softness, color, motion, shadows, midtones, highlights, gauge; a loupe at 100, 200 or 400 percent
Vignette
Amount, reach, feather, falloff, roundness, stretch, angle, highlights, shadows, color, tint, hue; placed on a picture of the frame
Layers
Add layer lays another look over the one in hand
Saved looks
The whole grade, kept for every project

Scopes

Blick Scope
Color and light in one drawing, a plume for each clip; Close up; against a reference; pushes Warmth, Tint and Exposure when dragged
And
Waveform, Parade, Vector, Histogram, CIE 1931, Numbers
Shown
Any number at once, at the foot of the window in Prepare and in Grade; each can be zoomed into

Good to know

A LUT
Holds the look of all clips. Finish, grain and vignette are in renders and stills, not in a LUT. Where scenes wear different looks, renders carry each scene's look
Skin
What Blick cares for as skin is a range of warm hues, not a face it has recognised. Wood and sand of that color are looked after too
Scenes
Which clips are one scene is judged by their colors, not by what is in them. You can always make a scene yourself
Sound
Finished files have their sound. A sequence sent back to Premiere Pro or Resolve places the picture only; the project for Final Cut Pro carries both
